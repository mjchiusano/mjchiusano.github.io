Coming August 17, 2027: Dash, Drive, Deliver: My Year in America's Gig Economy. In the tradition of Barbara Ehrenreich and Eric Schlosser, intrepid reporter Mark Chiusano spends a year as a gig worker for a deep dive into the underbelly of American labor, highlighting how app work is reshaping our society and coming soon to a job near you.

Mark is also the author of The Fabulist: The Lying, Hustling, Grifting, Stealing, and Very American Legend of George Santos, an "essential read for those seeking to understand the complex tapestry of truth and fabrication that defines the American political landscape" (The Advocate).

And the story collection Marine Park, a 2015 PEN/Hemingway Award Honorable Mention and "cult classic" (Our Town); "One of the most subtle, tender, emotionally powerful books that I’ve read in a long time . . . A stunning debut” (Saïd Sayrafiezadeh).
